**Mood Board**

*Ideas*
- AI-terity, Artificial Intelligence (AI) musical instrument - https://youtu.be/qlNd-h0rI2k?si=4ktUsyfNzihlAIkt
- Air Guitar: Using TinyML to Identify Complex Gestures (Harvard CS249R) - https://youtu.be/PVk9RUW1Hwo?si=U6_LM8YhS1ieXGsz
- Body attached device that adapts user movement as inputs in order to produce sounds.
- The videos and file are where the inspiration comes from, primarily the Air Guitar youtube video. The idea was already in my head about being able to produce sounds based off the tracking of movements,
  and that video helped me visualize the direction in which I would want to take things with this project.
- Pitch, volume, and/or key changes depending on hand positioning and movement.
- Control of varius musical componets to allow for unique sounds.
