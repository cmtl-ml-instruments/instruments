# Mood board

Links, not uploads. Drop things in as you find them — this is a scrapbook, not
a document. Say a line about why each one is here; in a month you won't
remember.

## How it looks and feels

Objects, materials, instruments, anything worn or held.

-

## How it sounds

Recordings, synths, pieces, sounds you want in the room.

-

## How it works

Sensors, projects, papers, videos of something doing what you want yours to do.
